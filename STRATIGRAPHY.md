# STRATIGRAPHY:

## 7 PRINCPLES (IMP):
1. SUPERPOSITION
2. ORIGINAL HORIZONTALITY:
    A. USING MUD CRACKS: ALWAYS OPEN UP IN UPWARDS DIRECTION
    B. SIZE DECREASE OF GRAINS: SIZE USUALLY DECRESES UPWARDS
3. ORIGNIAL LATERAL CONTINUITY:
    A. PINCHING OUT
    B. LAYERING AGAINST UNEVEN ROCKS
    C. EROSION
    D. CUT BY A FAULT
4. UNIFORMITARIANISM
5. BIOLOGICAL SUCCESION (ALSO KNOWN AS LAW OF FAUNAL SUCCESSION)
6. CROSS CUTTING
7. INCLUSIONS

## STRATIGRAPHIC BORDERS:
1. INTRUSIVE:
    A. SILLS (CONCORDANT)
    B. DYKES (DISCORDANT)
2. DEPOSITIONAL CONTACT:
    A. VERTICAL:
        (1) CONFIRMABLE
        (2) UNCONFIRMABLE
    B. LATERAL:
        (1) ABRUPT
        (2) GRADATIONAL
        (3) INTERTOUNGING
        (4) WEDGE

4. NONCONFIRMITY: SEDIMENTARY ROCKS ARE DEPOSITED OVER IGNEOUS OR METAMORPHIC BASES
